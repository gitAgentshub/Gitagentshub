About Git Agents Hub

Git Agents Hub is an independent, free-to-use AI image generation tool.

We built Git Agents Generate for anyone who wants to turn a text prompt into an image quickly — no account, no paywall, and no complicated setup. Pick a model, set your ratio, quality, and style, and generate.

What We Offer

  • Multiple AI models — Flux, Zimage, GPT Image, Kontext, Seedream 5, and Nano Banana, each suited to different styles and use cases.
  • Custom settings — square, landscape, or portrait ratios; Standard, HD, or Ultra quality; and style presets like Anime, Realistic, 3D, Pixel Art, and Cyberpunk.
  • No signup required — generate, download, and share images directly from your browser.

Who It's For

Git Agents Generate is built for indie hackers, designers, content creators, and anyone who needs a quick AI-generated image — for a project, a post, or just to experiment — without signing up for yet another paid tool.

How It Works

Prompts are sent to third-party AI image generation providers to render your image. We don't require an account and we don't store your prompts or generated images on our servers beyond what's needed to serve the result back to your browser.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 29, 2026

What we collect

We do not require an account to use Git Agents Generate. We do not collect names, emails, or personal identifiers. The text prompts you enter are sent to third-party AI image generation providers solely to render your requested image.

Third-party services

Image generation is powered by third-party AI providers (such as Pollinations AI). Your prompt text is transmitted to these providers to generate the image; we don't control their retention practices and recommend avoiding sensitive or personal information in your prompts.

Local storage

Your generation history is saved locally in your browser (not on our servers) so you can revisit past results. Clearing your browser data will remove this history. It is never shared with us or anyone else.

Cookies & analytics

We do not use tracking cookies or third-party advertising. Basic, anonymized traffic analytics may be used to understand overall site usage.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the product evolves. Continued use of Git Agents Generate after changes means you accept the updated policy.
